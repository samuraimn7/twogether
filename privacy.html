Privacy
Policy

Effective June 30, 2026

Twogether is a private weekly check-in for two people. We built it to be intimate and secure, and we collect as little as possible. This policy explains what we handle and why.

What we collect

  • Your email address — used only to sign you in and secure your account.
  • Your name — the display name you choose when pairing, shown to you and your partner.
  • Your check-in answers — the text you write in your weekly check-ins.
  • Basic technical data — standard information (like a session token) required to keep you signed in.

How we use it

Only to run the app: to sign you in, connect you with your one paired partner, sync your check-ins, and show your shared archive. That's it.

Who can see your entries

Your check-in answers are shared with only your paired partner, and only after you have both submitted for that week — that's the whole point of Twogether. Enforced at the database level, no one else — including other users — can read your entries.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use it for advertising.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Where it's stored

Your data is stored securely with our infrastructure provider (Supabase) and protected by row-level security, so each account can only access its own couple's data.

Your choices

You can request a copy of your data or permanent deletion of your account and check-ins at any time by emailing us. We'll action deletion requests promptly.

Children

Twogether is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and post the new version here.
