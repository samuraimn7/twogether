## Description
```
Twogether is a private weekly ritual for two people who want to stay close.

Each week, you and your partner answer the same handful of prompts — on your own, honestly. Here's the twist: neither of you can see the other's answers until you've both finished. No peeking, no influencing. Once you're both done, your reflections unlock side by side.

It's a small, judgment-free space to check in on what matters — how you're feeling, what you appreciated, what you're looking forward to — and to actually hear each other.

WHY COUPLES LOVE IT
• Answer independently — replies stay hidden until you both submit, so every answer is real
• A gentle weekly rhythm that keeps you connected, without pressure
• Add a photo to each check-in to capture the moment
• Look back anytime through your shared archive of weeks together
• Pick up where you left off — drafts save and follow you across your devices

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Twogether is built for exactly two people. You pair once with a simple invite code, and your space stays just yours. Your partner can't read your answers until they've written their own, and no one else can ever see your check-ins.

SIMPLE TO START
1. Create your space and share your invite code
2. Your partner joins — you're paired
3. Answer this week's prompts, then reveal together

Sign in in seconds with email or Google. No passwords, no noise — just the two of you.

Make checking in with each other a habit worth keeping. Twogether.
```
